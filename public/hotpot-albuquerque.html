Hot pot in Albuquerque: bubbling broth, everything in

The pot side of Blacksheep — all-you-can-eat hot pot cooked at your table, inside Cottonwood Mall. Cold evenings and big appetites, this is the pot for.

How the pot runs

The broth stays bubbling all night

A pot of broth arrives bubbling at your table and stays that way. Whatever is coming off the pass goes in, cooks in front of you, and comes out straight onto the table — no waiting for courses, no kitchen timing your evening for you.

Hot pot is built the same way Blacksheep runs everything: all-you-can-eat, around one table, good times included. It pairs with the grill side of the house on the same visit.

Good to know

Questions people ask

What is hot pot and how does it work here?

A pot of bubbling broth sits at your table while you cook everything in it. At Blacksheep it runs all-you-can-eat — the broth keeps bubbling and the refills keep coming.

Is the hot pot also all-you-can-eat?

Yes. Blacksheep is an all-you-can-eat Korean BBQ and hot pot spot — the refill format applies to the pot as well as the grill.

Where do I get hot pot in Albuquerque?

Inside Cottonwood Mall, Suite G217, 10000 Coors Bypass NW — open Mon–Thu 11:00 AM–10:00 PM, Fri–Sat until 11:00 PM, Sun 11:00 AM–10:00 PM.

Do I need a reservation for a hot pot table?

Walk-ins are welcome, and the Google listing has a Reserve a table action if you would rather lock one in. For a big group, call (505) 407-2915 first.

Also at the table: the grill

Sizzling table grills run all-you-can-eat too — pick the fire, pick the broth, or do both on one visit.

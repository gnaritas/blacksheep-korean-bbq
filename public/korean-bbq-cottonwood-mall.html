All-you-can-eat Korean BBQ at Cottonwood Mall

Sizzling grills at the table, refills on tap, Albuquerque mall food-court distance from everywhere west of the river — Suite G217, open seven days a week from 11 AM.

How the grill runs

Round the fire — you cook, we keep it coming

Korean BBQ is not something that arrives plated and cold. At Blacksheep the grill sits at your table, the meat hits it sizzling, and you grill exactly how you like it. When the plate is empty, the next one is already on the way — this is the all-you-can-eat part.

It is built for shared tables: families off the food court, birthday dinners, a big table of friends after errands at the mall. The format keeps everyone in the same seat and the same story.

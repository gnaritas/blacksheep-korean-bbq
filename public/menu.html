The format: grill, pot, table — all day long

Blacksheep runs all-you-can-eat Korean BBQ and hot pot. That one sentence is the whole menu philosophy — everything else happens at your table.

At the grill

Sizzling table grills, Korean BBQ style

Meat hits hot grills at your table and comes off sizzling — you cook, you eat, you order the next round. This is all-you-can-eat Korean BBQ the way it is meant to run: the fire stays lit and the plate keeps coming.

Full details on the dedicated page: All-You-Can-Eat Korean BBQ at Cottonwood Mall.

At the pot

Bubbling broth, everything in

Hot pot is the other half of the house: a pot of bubbling broth at the table, everything cooked in and eaten straight from the pot. Cold day, big group, or just a better dinner — the pot is the move.

Full details on the dedicated page: Hot Pot in Albuquerque.

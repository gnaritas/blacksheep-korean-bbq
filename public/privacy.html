Privacy — plain and short

This site has a contact form, a phone number, and a map pin. Here is exactly what happens with each.

What this site collects

Only what you choose to send us

When you fill in the contact form, the name, phone number, optional email, and message you type are sent to Blacksheep Korean BBQ & Hotpot so the team can answer your question about tables, groups, or the menu. That submission is the only information the site collects from you.

The site sets no cookies, runs no third-party scripts, and does no advertising tracking. There is no account system and nothing to consent to beyond choosing to call or send the form.

The Visit page embeds a Google Maps map so you can find Cottonwood Mall; loading that map means your browser talks to Google for the map tiles. The map does not collect anything on behalf of this site.

We do not sell information gathered here. If you emailed or messaged us and want it removed, call (505) 407-2915 and say the word.
